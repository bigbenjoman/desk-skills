Add a Claude login
desk never sees your password or token. You sign in with Claude Code's own login, and the login
keeps its own folder, so its sessions and settings stay apart from your others.
 
1 In another terminal, run:
CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=~/.claude-logins/login-5 claude
then type /login and sign in with the account you want to add.
2 Come back here. desk-q finds it within a minute, checks it isn't an account already listed,
and shows its email and plan. It starts on standby: press space to put it in rotation.
 
A Codex login works the same way, with CODEX_HOME=~/.codex-logins/login-2 codex login.
