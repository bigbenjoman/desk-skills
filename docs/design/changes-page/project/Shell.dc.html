How desk decides

A lever being tested is on for half of new tickets and off for the other half. Once 30 tickets on each side have finished, desk keeps the lever if those tickets cost less and nothing got worse: tickets accepted, fix tries, failed checks and reverts. Otherwise it turns the lever off.
