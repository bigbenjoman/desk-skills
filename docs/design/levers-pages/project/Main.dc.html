A lever being tested is on for half of new tickets and off for the other half. Once 30 tickets on each side have finished (40 for levers that touch review), desk keeps the lever if those tickets cost less and nothing got worse: tickets accepted, fix tries, failed CI runs, serious review findings, problems after merge and hours parked. If any of those gets worse, desk turns the lever off. A check with no estimate yet never turns a lever off.
